What happened in four days

From the first commit to a public repository: 18 November 2026, 13:57, to midday on 22 November

18–20 Novabout two days

First version: an interpreterCodex

Starting from one ELF program, then Linux system calls, Binder, graphics and touch. Android 17 reaches its home screen and installs APKs. Boot on an iPhone 15 Pro: 606 seconds.

Night of 20 Novinto the 21st

Review, and a new routeClaude

The verdict: an interpreter cannot get there. Small experiments on real phones first, to check that rebuilding Android and translating it ahead of time can work.

21 Novdaytime

Native code boots the whole systemClaude

First full boot on an iPhone 11: 110 seconds, then 34 once a missing daemon is added. On an iPhone 15 Pro: 11 seconds, then 6.5. The download goes from 1.76 GB to under 1 GB.

Night of 21 Novto the morning

Making it usableClaude

Shared storage, network, home-screen widgets, video and sound. Fifteen open-source apps installed. Boot settles at 8 to 10 seconds.

22 Novmidday

ReleaseClaude

One full run from a fresh install on an iPhone 17 Pro, an IPA, and the repository made public under Apache-2.0.
