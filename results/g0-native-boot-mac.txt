The Mac probe has no graphics backend, so SurfaceFlinger aborts ("no suitable EGLConfig found") each time
it starts, and init restarts the zygote with it. The boot therefore never gets past the start of
system_server here; what the lines below show is how long the stages before that take. The first zygote is
killed by that restart while it preloads, the second one finishes.
