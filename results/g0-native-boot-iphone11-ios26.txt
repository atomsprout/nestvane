---- time slices and memory accounting ----
The time slice signal never reached its handler on the phone: the guest ran on a dispatch queue's worker
thread, and Darwin does not deliver pthread_kill to those. The app now runs the guest on a thread of its
own. After that: preemptions 2336 of 30491 signals (10546 handled, 1212 outside guest code).
The stage times did not change with it (StartPackageManagerService 11.8 s, StartSystemUI 31.1 s,
USER_UNLOCKED 60.0 s in that run); the process was again gone after about 60 s, with a physical footprint
of 2,445,257,344 bytes in the last snapshot.

Where the memory is, from the snapshot at 30 s of the run before (footprint 1,855,034,008 bytes:
internal 692,879,360, compressed 1,121,058,816). Host pages per guest address space, by vm_region_64; pages
that processes share after fork are counted in each of them, so the rows add up to more than the footprint:

The zygote and the processes forked from it hold most of it. How much of a forked process's share is
really its own, and how iOS charges pages shared after fork, has not been separated yet.

---- the launcher is displayed ----
Further changes: large writable files keep only written pages (property areas are 128 KiB each and
nearly empty; 250 MB less on the Mac at 5 s), writable private file mappings are copy-on-write views of
the file, the signal return trampoline has an address inside the table of translated code (a handler's
return to it was a jump to address 0, which iOS answers with SIGKILL "CODESIGNING Invalid Page"), and the
guest's build.prop has ro.config.low_ram=true.
