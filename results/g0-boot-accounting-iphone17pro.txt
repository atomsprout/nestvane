Read as: system_server is a quarter of the guest CPU; the media provider is as large as SystemUI and the
launcher together, and like every app that lives in an APEX module it has no compiled code in the image;
52 processes share one host thread, and switching between them cost the scheduler 2.2 s.
After this run the scheduler was changed to scan timers every 200 microseconds instead of at every switch:
the next relaunch on this phone displayed the launcher after 12,500 ms.
